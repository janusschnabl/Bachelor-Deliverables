RegexToENFA:

a|b|c	:associativity
a|bc*	:precedence
a|	:Correct usage of the empty regex in combination with other productions
(a|b)*abc+() : something using every production
""	:empty
a	:literal
abcdef	:concat
a|b	:union
a*	:kleene
a+	:kleene2
(a|b)c	:grouping
a|a	:not collapsing semantically equivalent branches


RegexToNFA:
a	:baseline, nothing to do
""	:acceptance propogation and pruning
ab	:single epsilon bridge
abc	:multiple epsilon bridge (before and after move)
a|b	:branching epsilons
a*	:backwards propogation of accepting states
(a|b)c	:combined example
(a|b)*abc+() :Large combined example
((a|b)c)*|() :Large combine example with different structure	

RegexToDFA:

""	: Single state
ab	: simple multistate subset
a|b	: simple branching
a|a	: distinct NFA states combined into one subset behavior
a*	:looping accepting subsets
abcdef	: Large alphabet forcing many transitions
(a|b)c	:branching coming together
a(a|d)	:branching not in the beginning
(a|b)*abc+() 	: Large combined example
((a|)b)*c	: Large combined example
